Our Facilities

Select any facility to learn more about what's on offer.

Fine Dining

Seasonal tasting menus, heritage ingredients, and a cellar curated over decades.

Our award-winning restaurant, The Orangery, serves refined seasonal tasting menus under the direction of head chef Marcus Ainsley. A private dining room accommodates up to twelve guests, and our sommelier curates pairings from a cellar of over four hundred labels.

  • Hours: Breakfast 7–10am · Dinner 6:30–10pm
  • Dress code: Smart / smart casual
  • Reservations: Recommended for dinner

Luxury Suites

Forty individually designed suites with handcrafted furnishings and marble bathrooms.

Each of our forty suites has been individually designed, featuring handcrafted furnishings, marble bathrooms, and Egyptian cotton linens. In-room amenities include a Nespresso machine, complimentary mini bar, and bespoke toiletries commissioned from a local perfumer.

  • Turn-down: Nightly from 6pm
  • Room service: 24 hours
  • Housekeeping: Daily with on-request top-up

Spa & Wellness

A tranquil spa with steam rooms, indoor pool, and a full menu of signature treatments.

Our subterranean spa offers six treatment rooms, a eucalyptus steam room, Finnish sauna, and a heated indoor pool lit by candlelight in the evenings. Signature treatments include the Ivory Ritual — a two-hour journey of exfoliation, massage, and restorative facial.

  • Hours: Daily 7am–9pm
  • Pool access: Complimentary for guests
  • Treatments: Bookable via concierge

Concierge & Location

Moments from Belfast's cultural quarter, with a 24-hour concierge to arrange anything you need.

Set in the heart of Belfast's cultural quarter, The Ivory House is a short walk from the city's finest galleries, theatres, and restaurants. Our concierge team is available around the clock to arrange transport, tickets, private tours, and bespoke excursions across Northern Ireland.

  • Concierge: 24 hours
  • Valet parking: On request
  • Airport transfer: Complimentary for Signature guests
